Cookies used by the application

Strictly necessary
Django session cookie

Keeps users signed in and supports account workflows.

Strictly necessary
Django CSRF cookie and token

Protects forms from cross-site request forgery.

Strictly necessary
Cookie preference cookie

Remembers the analytics choice you make. The preference is stored for 180 days.

Analytics
Google Analytics, if configured and enabled

Measures product usage. Analytics is loaded only after you accept it or save preferences with analytics enabled.

The current application does not use advertising pixels, embedded maps, video embeds, chat widgets, or social widgets.

Manage your choice

Optional analytics are off by default. You can accept or reject analytics, or manage your preferences in the cookie panel. To change a saved choice later, use Cookie settings in the site footer.
