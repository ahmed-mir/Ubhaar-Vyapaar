Consent approach

Because the current site does not use optional analytics or marketing cookies, it does not display a blanket cookie-acceptance banner. Strictly necessary session and CSRF cookies should not be blocked because they are required for login, forms, and security.

If optional analytics, marketing, or third-party embeds are added later, they should be placed behind an explicit consent mechanism and should not run before the relevant consent category is granted.
