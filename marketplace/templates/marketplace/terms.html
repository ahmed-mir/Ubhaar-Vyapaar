Payments

When enabled, milestone checkout uses Razorpay Test Mode only. Sandbox transactions are not live payments. The platform does not accept live payments or provide escrow or settlement.

Manufacturer payout and milestone release remain simulated. Test payment records confirm only a sandbox transaction and do not represent money paid to a Manufacturer.

Accounts and roles

Users are responsible for keeping account credentials secure and for providing accurate account information. Buyer accounts can browse, save listings, request quotes, make eligible fixed-price purchases, accept quotations, manage orders, message manufacturers, and leave completed-order reviews.

Manufacturer accounts can create capacity listings, maintain business profiles, respond to quote requests, send quotations, manage order milestones, and message buyers.

Listings, quotations, and orders

Manufacturers are responsible for the accuracy of listings, prices, lead times, capacity statements, images, and quotations. Buyers are responsible for providing practical quote requirements and delivery information.

Accepting a quotation creates an order with locked terms and milestones. Eligible public fixed-price listings may also create an order through Buy Now. Neither flow itself takes payment. Test Mode milestone payments, when enabled, are sandbox only; Manufacturer payout and milestone release remain simulated.

An eligible non-Sample Manufacturer profile displays a Verified badge only when its profile verification flag is approved. The badge is not a certification, endorsement, or product or transaction guarantee. Read what Verified means.

Communication and reviews

Order messages and reviews should be accurate, lawful, and relevant to the marketplace workflow. Users should not post confidential, abusive, misleading, infringing, or unlawful content.

Prohibited conduct

  • Submitting false business, listing, quotation, or review information.
  • Requesting or sharing payment card, UPI, bank credential, or government ID information through the platform.
  • Uploading unlawful, infringing, unsafe, or misleading content.
  • Attempting to access another user's account, order, messages, or notifications.
  • Using the platform to spam, harass, scrape, or disrupt service availability.

User-generated content and intellectual property

Users remain responsible for content they submit, including listing text, images, messages, quote requirements, and reviews. Users should submit only content they have the right to use.

Account suspension or termination

The application does not provide a public account suspension or appeals process.

Availability, liability, and disputes

The service may change, pause, or become unavailable. Users should resolve commercial terms directly with one another before accepting a quotation. These terms do not specify a governing law or formal dispute-resolution process.
