What does Verified mean?

The Verified badge appears only for non-sample manufacturer accounts whose verification flag is approved. A KYC status by itself does not display the badge.

A missing badge means that no verified state is being displayed. Verification is not a certification or a guarantee of quality, delivery, payment, or a particular outcome.
