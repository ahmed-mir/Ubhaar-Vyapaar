Verification status

What Verified means

The Verified badge is shown for a non-sample manufacturer profile when its public verification flag is approved. A KYC status by itself does not display the badge.

The platform does not publish a checklist of checks represented by this status. Review the manufacturer and listing details relevant to your order.

Read the status carefully

What Verified does not mean

The status is not a certification or a guarantee of a product or transaction.

  • It is not government or legal accreditation.
  • It does not certify product quality or inspect each product.
  • It does not guarantee delivery, payment, or a commercial outcome.
  • It is not an endorsement, insurance, or a guarantee against fraud.

For Buyers

Checks before you agree

  • Confirm the specifications and quantity you need.
  • Review the quotation and clarify its terms with the Manufacturer.
  • Discuss delivery expectations and verify information important to your purchase.

For Manufacturers

Keep listing and order details clear

  • Provide accurate business, listing, capacity, price, and lead-time information.
  • Respond to quote requests with accurate quotation terms.
  • Use order messages and milestones to communicate order progress.

Need to raise a concern?

Contact the platform

Use the Contact form for a concern about a listing, account, or platform use. For an accepted order, you can also contact the other participant through its order messages. No response time or dispute outcome is promised here.

Sample listings and payment states
