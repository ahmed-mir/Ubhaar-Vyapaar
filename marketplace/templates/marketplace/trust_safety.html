What does Verified mean?

The Verified badge appears only when the manufacturer account’s verification state supports it. That state is controlled by the account verification and KYC records; it is not inferred from listing text, reviews, seed data, or a manufacturer name.

A missing badge means that no verified state is being displayed. Verification is not a certification or a guarantee of quality, delivery, payment, or a particular outcome.
