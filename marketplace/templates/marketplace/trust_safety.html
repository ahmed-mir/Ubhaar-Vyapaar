What Verified means

The Verified badge appears only for non-sample manufacturer accounts whose verification flag is approved. A KYC status by itself does not display the badge.

What Verified does not mean

Verification is not a certification, product inspection, quality guarantee, delivery guarantee, payment guarantee, endorsement, or insurance. It does not guarantee a particular transaction outcome.

Sample data

Listings marked Sample are illustrative. They do not accept quote requests. Sample manufacturers are not shown with the Verified badge, and sample ratings and reviews are hidden.
