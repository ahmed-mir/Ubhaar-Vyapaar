Why it exists

Start with practical manufacturing details

Buyers looking for production capacity need to understand details such as process, minimum order quantity, location, price, and lead time. Manufacturers may have capacity they can describe to prospective Buyers. The marketplace brings listing information and quotation requests into one place.

What the platform does

A place to discover, quote, and follow orders

  • Manufacturers publish capacity listings with available practical details.
  • Buyers browse listings and send quote requests.
  • Manufacturers respond with quotations for Buyers to review.
  • Accepting a quotation creates an order with milestones and order messages.
See how the workflow works

Who it is for

Two sides of a manufacturing conversation

Buyers

Businesses looking for manufacturing capacity can compare available listing details and request quotations.

Manufacturers

Manufacturers can describe capacity, respond to quote requests, and follow accepted orders.

Current stage

An early-access pilot

Marketplace discovery, quote requests, quotations, orders, milestones, and order messaging are implemented. Sample listings are shown for illustration and do not accept quote requests. Funding and release labels are simulated; the platform does not process real payments.
