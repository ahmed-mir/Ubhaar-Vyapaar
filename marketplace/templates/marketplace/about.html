What the marketplace does

Buyers can search listings, compare the information manufacturers provide, and request quotations. Manufacturers can publish capacity, send quotations, and manage accepted orders through messages and production milestones.

Listings show details such as category or process, location, MOQ, price, and lead time when those values are available.

Who it serves

The marketplace is for businesses looking for manufacturing capacity and manufacturers with capacity to publish.

Early access and sample listings

Ubhaar-Vyapaar is in early access. Some listings are marked Sample and are shown for illustration; sample listings do not accept quote requests.

Order funding and release labels are simulated. The platform does not process payments or escrow.
