Why it exists

Start with practical manufacturing details

Buyers looking for production capacity need practical information such as process, minimum order quantity, location, price, and lead time. The marketplace brings those listing details together so Buyers can assess available manufacturing options.

What the platform does

Discover capacity and start a commercial conversation

  • Manufacturers describe capacity they can offer.
  • Buyers can choose Buy Now on eligible fixed-price listings or Request Quote for custom terms.
  • Orders, messages, and production milestones stay connected in the marketplace.
See the Buyer and Manufacturer journeys

Who it is for

Two sides of a manufacturing conversation

Buyers

Businesses looking for manufacturing capacity can compare listing details and choose a direct fixed-price purchase where available or request custom terms.

Manufacturers

Manufacturers can describe their capacity and manage quote requests and eligible orders after their business profile is reviewed.

Current stage

A marketplace in pilot

Account registration is available now. Manufacturer listings become public after profile verification. Sample listings are illustrative and cannot be purchased or quoted.
