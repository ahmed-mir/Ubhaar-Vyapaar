About the marketplace

Manufacturing capacity, easier to find.

Ubhaar-Vyapaar brings capacity listings and quotation requests into one marketplace workflow.

For buyers

Search listings by capability, category, location, minimum order quantity, price, and lead time. Review the manufacturer profile and its verification status before requesting a quote.

Browse capacity

For manufacturers

Create a business profile, publish production capacity, and respond to buyer requests with quotations.

List capacity

Orders and funding

Accepted quotations can become orders with messages and production milestones. Funding and release states are simulated workflow markers; the platform does not process real payments or provide escrow.
