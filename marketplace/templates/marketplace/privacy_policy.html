Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account data: username, email address, optional phone number, password hash, role, registration consent timestamps and policy versions.
  • Manufacturer profile data: business name, business description, city, state, verification flags, and KYC review status.
  • Marketplace listing data: title, category, description, capabilities, machine type, materials, price, MOQ, production days, capacity, cluster, availability status, and optional listing image.
  • Buyer workflow data: saved listings, quote-request quantity, requirements, and delivery location.
  • Contact enquiries: name, email address, role, and message submitted through the Contact page.
  • Early-access interest: email address, buyer or manufacturer role, optional note, and submission time.
  • Commercial workflow data: quotations, accepted terms, demo order records, simulated milestone funding/release states, messages, notifications, and reviews.
  • Authentication/security data: Django session and CSRF cookies, password reset email tokens, and server/security logs generated by the hosting environment.

We do not request government ID numbers, card details, UPI IDs, bank credentials, or KYC document uploads.

How information is used

  • To create buyer and manufacturer accounts.
  • To show manufacturer capacity and verification status to buyers.
  • To support quote requests, quotations, demo orders, messages, notifications, and reviews.
  • To verify or review manufacturer business profiles before public listing visibility.
  • To send password reset and system emails.
  • To respond to contact enquiries sent through the Contact page.
  • To manage early-access interest. Waitlist email addresses are not added to a marketing list automatically.
  • To protect accounts, prevent unauthorized access, and maintain application security.

Consent and account registration

Account registration requires users to agree to the Terms & Conditions and acknowledge this Privacy Policy. The application records the timestamp and policy versions for that account event. Marketing consent is not collected by the current application.

Cookies and browser preferences

The current site uses strictly necessary Django session and CSRF cookies, plus a cookie preference cookie after a user makes a choice. That preference is stored for 180 days. Optional Google Analytics is loaded only when a measurement ID is configured and the user accepts analytics. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Service providers

Listing images may be stored and delivered through the media storage provider. Password reset and system emails may be sent through an email service provider.

These providers may receive data necessary to provide their service, such as public listing image files and metadata for Cloudinary, or email addresses and email contents for SMTP delivery.

Data retention

The current application does not implement automated deletion or retention schedules. Account, order, quotation, milestone, message, review, notification, listing, and contact-submission records may be retained until an administrator changes or deletes them. Early-access interest records may be retained until an administrator changes or deletes them.

This policy does not set a specific retention period for each record type.

Security

The application uses Django authentication, password hashing, CSRF protection, session cookies, HTTPS-focused production security settings, upload validation for listing images, and role-based access checks for buyer/manufacturer workflows.

User rights and requests

Users can change their password from account settings. Manufacturers can edit their business profile. A safe self-service account deletion workflow is not currently implemented because deleting accounts may affect business records, orders, messages, and reviews.
