Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account data: username, email address, optional phone number, password hash, role, registration consent timestamps and policy versions.
  • Manufacturer profile data: business name, business description, city, state, verification flags, and KYC review status.
  • Marketplace listing data: title, category, description, capabilities, machine type, materials, price, MOQ, production days, capacity, cluster, availability status, and optional listing image.
  • Buyer workflow data: saved listings, quote-request quantity, requirements, and delivery location.
  • Contact enquiries: name, email address, role, and message submitted through the Contact page.
  • Pilot update request: email address, Buyer or Manufacturer role, optional note, and submission time.
  • Commercial workflow data: quotations, accepted terms, direct and quotation-based orders, milestone states, Razorpay Test Mode transaction identifiers and statuses when enabled, messages, notifications, and reviews.
  • Authentication/security data: Django session and CSRF cookies, password reset email tokens, and server/security logs generated by the hosting environment.

We do not request or store government ID numbers, card details, UPI PINs, bank credentials, or KYC document uploads. Razorpay Test Mode checkout fields are handled by Razorpay in its sandbox.

How information is used

  • To create buyer and manufacturer accounts.
  • To show manufacturer capacity and verification status to buyers.
  • To support quote requests, quotations, direct and quotation-based orders, Test Mode payment records when enabled, messages, notifications, and reviews.
  • To verify or review manufacturer business profiles before public listing visibility.
  • To send password reset and system emails.
  • To respond to contact enquiries sent through the Contact page.
  • To send pilot updates requested through the homepage form. Waitlist email addresses are not added to a marketing list automatically.
  • To protect accounts, prevent unauthorized access, and maintain application security.

Consent and account registration

Account registration requires users to agree to the Terms & Conditions and acknowledge this Privacy Policy. The application records the timestamp and policy versions for that account event. Marketing consent is not collected by the current application.

Cookies and browser preferences

The current site uses strictly necessary Django session and CSRF cookies, plus a cookie preference cookie after a user makes a choice. That preference is stored for 180 days. Optional Google Analytics is loaded only when a measurement ID is configured and the user accepts analytics. See the Cookie Policy for details.

Service providers

Listing images may be stored and delivered through the media storage provider. Password reset and system emails may be sent through an email service provider.

These providers may receive data necessary to provide their service, such as public listing image files and metadata for Cloudinary, or email addresses and email contents for SMTP delivery.

Data retention

Users can export account data and close an eligible account from the signed-in Privacy and data page. Closure deactivates sign-in and anonymizes account/profile contact details. Manufacturer listings are unpublished. Active orders, open quote requests, or pending payment workflows must be resolved first.

Orders, quotations, milestones, payment history, reviews, and other records needed to preserve marketplace history may remain after closure, with account/profile identity anonymized where the application can do so safely. The application does not set a specific retention period for these records. Support handles requests because contact-submission records may be retained and Pilot update request records may be retained; the application does not set a specific retention period for either category. Requests about those records can be sent to the privacy/support contact above, or through Contact support.

Account closure does not automatically remove copies held by service providers or from backups, logs, or stored listing media. Provider retention and deletion settings are handled separately and may affect how long copies remain.

Security

The application uses Django authentication, password hashing, CSRF protection, session cookies, HTTPS-focused production security settings, upload validation for listing images, and role-based access checks for buyer/manufacturer workflows.

User rights and requests

Signed-in users can download a JSON export of their account and associated marketplace data, or request account closure through the Privacy and data page. Closure requires current-password confirmation. It may be deferred while marketplace obligations are active. Transaction and payment records are not erased as part of account closure.

Pilot-update requests do not currently have an automated unsubscribe control. To stop future pilot communications or request review of a contact/pilot submission, use the configured privacy/support contact or Contact support. Support may ask for information needed to verify the request.
