Listing prices and quotations

Manufacturers provide listing prices where available and set the terms in their quotations. Review the quotation with the manufacturer before accepting it.

Platform fees

The current application does not calculate or collect platform fees. No fixed or future platform fee schedule is published here.

Payments

The platform does not process real payments. Funding and release labels in the order workflow are simulated and do not mean that money is collected, held, moved, or released.

Questions about current pricing?

Use the Contact page for questions about the platform. For listing or quotation terms, speak with the other party before accepting.
