How sourcing works

A quieter way to move from search to production.

Ubhaar-Vyapaar keeps commercial signals, quote requests, order checkpoints, and reviews tied to the same manufacturing record.

  1. 01Find capacity

    Search by process, category, location, supplier, material, or cluster.

  2. 02Send requirements

    Share quantities, delivery needs, and production context through a quote request.

  3. 03Compare quotations

    Review offered unit price, production days, and manufacturer terms.

  4. 04Track production

    Accepted quotations create order milestones and simulated funding checkpoints.

  5. 05Complete and review

    Close the order workflow and leave feedback after production is complete.
