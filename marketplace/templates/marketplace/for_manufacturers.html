What you can do

Tools to manage production enquiries

  • List spare capacityDescribe your process, materials, location, and availability.
  • Set practical terms upfrontShow MOQ, price, lead time, and capacity where available.
  • Respond with a quotationReview buyer requirements and send your price and production time.
  • Follow accepted ordersUpdate milestones and keep order-specific communication together.

Getting started

Set up your workspace, then respond

  1. 01

    Create a manufacturer account

    Enter your account and business details in the existing registration flow.

  2. 02

    Complete your profile and add capacity

    Use the listing form to describe the products or services you can provide.

  3. 03

    Review quote requests

    Check requested quantities, requirements, and delivery locations before quoting.

  4. 04

    Quote, then manage accepted orders

    Send your terms; if a buyer accepts, use the order milestones and message thread to follow work.
