What a capacity listing describes

Manufacturers can share the details available in the listing form, such as category, machine or process, materials, location, price, minimum order quantity, lead time, capacity, availability, and an image.

Keep listing details current and accurate. Buyers use them to decide whether to ask for a quotation; the final commercial terms are set in the quotation.

Quote requests and accepted orders

Buyers send a quantity, requirements, and delivery location through a quote request. Manufacturers can respond with a quotation. When a buyer accepts it, the application creates an order with the accepted terms, messages, and production milestones.

Manufacturers can update milestone progress and communicate with the buyer through the order.

Verification status

The Verified badge appears only when a non-sample manufacturer profile has the authoritative verification flag. A KYC status alone does not display the badge. Verification is not a certification or a guarantee of quality, delivery, payment, or a particular outcome.

Some marketplace listings are marked Sample for illustration and do not accept quote requests.
