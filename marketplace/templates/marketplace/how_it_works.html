For buyers

Find a fit and request a quote

  1. 01

    Browse capacity

    Search active manufacturing listings by the details available on the marketplace.

  2. 02

    Review the listing

    Compare MOQ (minimum order quantity), price, lead time, location, and stated capacity.

  3. 03

    Request a quotation

    Send the quantity, requirements, and delivery location to the manufacturer.

  4. 04

    Review the quotation

    Check the manufacturer’s price, quantity, production time, and message.

  5. 05

    Decide and follow progress

    Accept or decline. Acceptance creates an order with agreed terms, milestones, and an order message thread.

For manufacturers

Publish capacity and respond

  1. 01

    Create an account

    Set up a manufacturer account and business profile.

  2. 02

    List manufacturing capacity

    Share listing details such as category, MOQ, lead time, location, and availability.

  3. 03

    Respond to quote requests

    Review buyer requirements and send a quotation with your terms.

  4. 04

    Manage accepted orders

    Use order messages and milestones to share progress with the buyer.

Listings from profiles awaiting verification remain hidden from public browsing until the profile is verified.
