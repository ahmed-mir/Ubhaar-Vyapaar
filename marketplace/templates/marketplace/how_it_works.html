For buyers

  1. Discover active manufacturing capacity in the marketplace.
  2. Review the listing's MOQ, price, lead time, capacity, availability, and manufacturer profile.
  3. Submit a quote request with quantity, requirements, and delivery location.
  4. Receive a quotation and review its terms.
  5. Accept or decline the quotation. Acceptance creates an order with the agreed terms.
  6. Use order messages and milestones to follow production progress.
  7. After the order is completed, submit a review under the existing review rules.

For manufacturers

  1. Publish manufacturing capacity in a listing.
  2. Review incoming quote requests and send quotations.
  3. Manage orders created when buyers accept quotations.
  4. Update production milestones and communicate through order messages.

Orders and payment status

Order messages and milestones track activity in the application. Funding and release labels are simulated workflow states. Ubhaar-Vyapaar does not process platform payments or escrow.
