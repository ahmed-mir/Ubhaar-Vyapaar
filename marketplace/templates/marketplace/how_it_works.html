How it works

From search to production updates.

Use the marketplace to compare listing terms and start a conversation with a manufacturer.

1. Discover capacity

Search and filter listings. Each listing shows its stated price, minimum order quantity, lead time, availability, and manufacturer profile.

2. Request and review a quote

Buyers send requirements to a manufacturer. The manufacturer can respond with a quotation for the buyer to review.

3. Follow the order

Accepting a quotation creates an order with messages and milestones. Funding and release labels are simulated; no real payment moves through the platform.

4. Complete and review

After completion, buyers can leave a review tied to their order.
