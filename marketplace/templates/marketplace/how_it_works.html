For buyers

Browse, choose a route, and follow the order

  1. 01

    Browse manufacturing capacity

    Search active listings and compare their stated commercial details.

  2. 02

    Choose Buy Now or Request Quote

    Buy Now is available on eligible fixed-price listings. Use Request Quote for custom quantities or terms.

  3. 03

    Confirm the order

    Confirm a direct purchase to create its order. For a quote request, review the Manufacturer's quotation; accepting it creates the order.

  4. 04

    Track the order and milestones

    Order participants can follow milestone updates and communicate in the order message thread.

For manufacturers

Complete your profile, then manage capacity

  1. 01

    Register and complete your business profile

    Provide your account and business details.

  2. 02

    Wait for profile review

    Public listing visibility follows the existing business verification process.

  3. 03

    List available capacity

    Describe the category, MOQ, lead time, location, and availability where applicable.

  4. 04

    Respond to demand

    Send quotations for Buyer requests and manage orders from eligible direct purchases.

  5. 05

    Manage orders and milestones

    Use order messages and milestone updates to share progress with the Buyer.

Listings from profiles awaiting verification remain hidden from public browsing until the profile is verified.

Two purchase paths

From listing to order updates

  1. Buy Now on an eligible fixed-price listing
  2. Buyer confirms the direct purchase
  3. Order and milestones

For custom terms, Request Quote → Manufacturer quotation → Buyer accepts → order and milestones.

Current pilot

What the platform supports today

  • Marketplace discovery and saved listings
  • Buy Now on eligible fixed-price listings
  • Quote requests and Manufacturer quotations
  • Accepted orders with production milestones
  • Order-specific messages and profile verification status

Sample listings are illustrative and cannot be purchased or quoted.
