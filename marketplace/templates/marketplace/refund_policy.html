No platform payment processing

Ubhaar-Vyapaar uses simulated funding and release states in demo order workflows. The platform does not charge, collect, hold, move, or release real payments and does not provide real escrow.

Because the platform does not process real payments, it does not process monetary refunds. This page does not set refund terms for commercial arrangements made directly between buyers and manufacturers.

Order cancellations

The application does not provide a self-service order cancellation workflow or define cancellation windows or penalties. Buyers and manufacturers should communicate about cancellation requests through their order messages.
