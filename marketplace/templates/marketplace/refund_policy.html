Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy describes the payment and cancellation functionality currently available on Ubhaar-Vyapaar.

No current platform payment processing

Ubhaar-Vyapaar currently uses simulated funding and release states for demo order workflows. No real payment is charged through the platform at present. No actual money is collected, held, moved, or released by Ubhaar-Vyapaar, and no real escrow account is currently provided.

Because the platform does not currently process real monetary payments, platform-processed monetary refunds are not currently performed.

Order cancellations

The application does not currently provide a self-service order cancellation workflow. Buyers and manufacturers should resolve cancellation requests directly with one another.
