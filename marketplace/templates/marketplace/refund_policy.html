Test payments and refunds

Razorpay Test Mode may be enabled for sandbox milestone payments only. It does not accept live payments. Manufacturer payout and milestone release remain simulated, and the platform does not provide escrow or settlement.

Ubhaar-Vyapaar does not offer live payment collection or monetary refunds through this test integration. This page does not set refund terms for commercial arrangements made directly between buyers and manufacturers.

Order cancellations

The application does not provide a self-service order cancellation workflow or define cancellation windows or penalties. Buyers and manufacturers should communicate about cancellation requests through their order messages.
