How can I purchase manufacturing capacity?

Use Buy Now on an eligible fixed-price listing, or select Request Quote for custom quantities or terms. Accepting a Manufacturer quotation creates an order.

See both Buyer and Manufacturer journeys.

What does Verified mean?

The badge appears only for non-sample manufacturer accounts whose verification flag is approved. Check each profile individually; listings without a badge should not be assumed verified.

What are Sample listings?

Sample listings demonstrate the marketplace and cannot be purchased or quoted. Buyers may save them while keeping their Sample label.
