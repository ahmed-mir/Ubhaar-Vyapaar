Help

Marketplace guidance.

Find answers about the current buyer and manufacturer workflows.

How do I request a quote?

Open a listing and select Request quote. Buyer account sign-in is required to send your requirements.

What does Verified mean?

The badge appears only for non-sample manufacturer accounts whose verification flag is approved. Check each profile individually; listings without a badge should not be assumed verified.

Can I pay through the platform?

No. Funding and release states are simulated workflow markers. Ubhaar-Vyapaar does not currently process payments or hold funds in escrow.
